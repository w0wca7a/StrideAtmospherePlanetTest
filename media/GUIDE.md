# Media guide

Delete this file once you've added your real media.

- **Motion sells.** A ~5 s GIF/MP4 of the asset *doing its thing* is worth ten screenshots.
  The store plays your **first animated media on card hover** — lead with it.
- **First image = money shot.** The first entry in `manifest.json`'s `media` array is what
  people see first on the detail page gallery.
- **Keep files < 10 MB** (they're served from your repo via raw.githubusercontent.com).
- Reference files here from the manifest with repo-relative paths: `media/wind.mp4`.
- `thumbnail.png` (~512×512) is separate: it's the card image and the link-preview image.
